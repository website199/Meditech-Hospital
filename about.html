About Our Hospital

Our Story

Meditech Hospital & Sports Injury Centre is a 30-bed hospital built to deliver advanced orthopaedic and dental care in Patna. Led by Dr. Ratnesh Singh (MS), a surgeon trained in the latest international techniques, we combine modern infrastructure with compassionate care.
